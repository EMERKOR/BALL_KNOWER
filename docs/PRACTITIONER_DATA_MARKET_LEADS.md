## 4for4 SharpStack

[Product description](https://www.4for4.com/sharpstack-landing) and [2026 walkthrough](https://www.4for4.com/sharpstack-walkthrough-plus-ev-arbitrage-and-pickem-tools) describe a live odds feed, +EV scanner, arbitrage and middles, prop/odds screens, market-derived player projections, pick'em optimizer, bet tracking, CLV and result views. The publisher says its fair odds come from multiple books and exchanges after removing hold and adjusting for book sharpness and historical efficiency. The walkthrough exposes sharp and target quote age, EV percentage, growth rate, Kelly suggestion, line and odds, and notes a 2026 odds-history popup. The landing page says 20+ books in one section and 30+ books/exchanges in its FAQ; treat precise coverage as unverified for the target NFL markets and jurisdiction.

**Classification:** commercial market-pricing/operations reference, not published implementation of an independent football forecast. The formula for book weights, efficiency adjustments, de-vigging by market, line interpolation, hold treatment for pushes, and any historical algorithm training data were not disclosed in these public pages. Do not import the “profitable” or “true odds” claims as verified results. A trial or purchase is not required for the documentation task.

**Audit:** request a sample or trial only if authorized/available; record NFL market/book coverage, timestamps and quote age, export/API and historical snapshot availability, price, terms, and whether result/CLV views preserve the offered line and price. For several accessible recommendations, independently recompute implied/no-vig probability and EV from displayed inputs and document any irreducible difference. Separate a market consensus benchmark from a genuinely independent model.

## Fantasy Points Data Suite 2.0

The [September 8, 2026 launch notice](https://newsletter.fantasypoints.com/p/fantasy-points-data-suite-2) lists 30+ research tables, player/teammate splits, matchup pages and coverage, formations, personnel, motion, pressure, routes and alignment. The [product page](https://fantasypointsdata.com/) describes more than 40 tools, 2,000 columns and 225,000 charted plays; passing/rushing/receiving filters, charted route separation (including untargeted routes), participation and shares, practice/injury designation, playcallers/coaches since 2021 and CSV table export. These counts and coverage are provider claims, not a verified data inventory. Charted data and derived XFP are different from a public, timestamped machine-learning training feed.

**Classification:** potential commercial feature/data source for team/QB/player/prop layers and source of domain definitions. Public pages do not establish an API, bulk licensing, full historical field coverage, actual release/revision timestamps, or the right to use exported CSV in an automated forecasting product. Injury reports in a current historical UI do not prove the original pregame version is preserved. The product page offers sign-in; subscription eligibility and terms must be checked rather than assumed.

**Audit:** obtain a data dictionary and sample exports for selected old and current weeks; inspect IDs, route/snaps denominators, missingness, charting latency, correction history and first-available timestamps. Ask provider about bulk/API access, licensing, archival as-of snapshots and reproducible extraction. Map every candidate field to a pregame known-at rule and compare its incremental held-out value to the simple baselines only after availability is proven.

## First-person sportsbook evidence

- [Circa on opening lines](https://www.circasports.com/blog/from-the-oddsmakers-setting-lines) (2019): describes an opener as a starting number, initially lower limits, skilled bets and subsequent market adjustment. It cautions against treating any opener or generic “Vegas” consensus as a unique truth label.
- [Circa on line moves](https://www.circasports.com/blog/from-the-experts-line-movement-causes) (2020): injuries, weather and the identity/information of bettors are cited as move drivers; a wager's size alone does not determine its informational value.
- [Circa director on limits](https://www.circasports.com/blog/art-of-setting-sports-betting-limits) (2020): describes standard limits, liquidity and using wagers to refine prices. This is an account of market formation, not a published coefficient or proof that all current Circa NFL markets have the same behavior.
- [Circa risk manager Dylan Sullivan on NFL props](https://www.circasports.com/blog/prop-betting-on-the-rise) (2023): says game sides/totals begin from team power-rating differences plus home/injury adjustments; prop menus and lines involve player role and matchup judgment. It does not disclose numeric ratings, prop distributions, training code or out-of-sample scores.
- [Pinnacle trading director Marco Blume interview and transcript](https://www.datacamp.com/podcast/data-science-gambling-and-bookmaking) (2019): describes sports modeling plus ticket-flow/risk work, testing models and refining prices based on informative bettors. This supports treating prices as an evolving market observation, without revealing Pinnacle's NFL pricing architecture or weights. [Pinnacle's margin explanation](https://www.pinnacle.com/betting-resources/en/educational/how-to-calculate-betting-margins/sz9jzegz3nn9fvwt) is a separate public pricing reference.

**Audit:** build a primary-source ledger of named sportsbook practitioners and their exact statements, dates, sport/market, disclosed equation or process, and missing detail. Search talks, interviews, operator posts and papers beyond these leads, including other major books, but do not extrapolate a soccer or trading example into an NFL model. Compare opening, decision-time and close quotes at identifiable books and limits, accounting for both point and price movements and pushes. A close can be a useful outcome-adjacent benchmark only when its time and market are explicit; it is not an earlier decision-time feature.
