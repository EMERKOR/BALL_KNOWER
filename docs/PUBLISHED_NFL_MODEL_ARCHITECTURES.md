# Published NFL model architectures: reproduction reference

Status: research inventory, 2026-09-27. This is a guide to what identifiable published code actually does, not a selection of Ball Knower's eventual model. Read the linked implementations before copying a coefficient or claiming a result. Existing v3 choices are hypotheses until reproduced against the same forecast targets, information cutoff, and baselines.

## Targets and directly inspectable examples

| Target | Published architecture and exact source | Data and fitted quantities | What it demonstrates; reproduction limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Game win probability | [FiveThirtyEight NFL Elo forecast.py](https://github.com/fivethirtyeight/nfl-elo-game/blob/master/forecast.py) | Sequential team Elo, home advantage, offseason reversion, margin-of-victory adjustment; source constants include K=20, home field=65 Elo, one-third reversion toward 1505. | Minimal game winner baseline with explicit updates. It does not itself price totals or a full integer margin distribution. Preserve chronology and verify treatment of ties and neutral sites. |
| Tuned game winner | [schwill2018 NFL Elo code](https://github.com/schwill2018/nfl_elo_project/blob/main/nfl_elo.py), [parameters](https://github.com/schwill2018/nfl_elo_project/blob/main/elo_parameters.csv) | Earlier-season grid fitting of K, home advantage, carryover and MOV scale; published 2026 row K=49, HFA=31, carryover=.65, MOV scale=2.2098. | A concrete example of how weights can be selected prospectively instead of guessed. Its reported scores are author results, not Ball Knower validation. Inspect its overtime partial-win convention before reproducing. |
| Game winner plus market | [nfelo model](https://github.com/greerreNFL/nfelo/blob/main/nfelo/Model/Nfelo.py), [config](https://github.com/greerreNFL/nfelo/blob/main/config.json), [market regression](https://github.com/greerreNFL/nfelo/blob/main/nfelo/Utilities/MarketRegression/regress_markets.py), [priors](https://github.com/greerreNFL/nfelo/blob/main/nfelo/Utilities/Priors/README.md), [training playbook](https://github.com/greerreNFL/nfelo/blob/main/training/TRAINING_PLAYBOOK.md) | Mean-reverted rating prior blended with preseason DVOA, win totals, unit ratings; QB and home effects; game updates and separate open/close market regression. Published config v4.3.1 includes reversion .5263, K 9.114, DVOA weight .3637, win-total weight .4496, unit weight .1811. | An implementation to trace field by field and reproduce with available inputs. Some upstream priors/third-party ratings may be inaccessible. The close-market path is only valid at close. Its [optimizer analysis](https://github.com/greerreNFL/nfelo/blob/main/nfelo/Optimizer/ANALYSIS_PLAYBOOK.md) distinguishes optimizer objective from evaluation. Its translation package [nfelotranslation](https://pypi.org/project/nfelotranslation/) derives discrete margin and cover/push probabilities and requires a separate dependency audit. |
| Margin and total distributions | [melo implementation](https://github.com/morelandjs/melo/blob/master/src/melo/melo.py), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.00527) | Threshold-specific Elo models across result margins and totals build cumulative distributions; sequential fitted updates, regression and a step parameter (code default k=.245). | A directly inspectable distribution architecture. Reproduce CDF monotonicity, integer PMF, push probability and held-out scoring; paper-level fitting is not prospective betting evidence. |
| Margin with football features | [kshreyan margin model](https://github.com/kshreyan/nfl-predict/blob/main/src/nfl/models/spread/margin_model.py), [config](https://github.com/kshreyan/nfl-predict/blob/main/configs/config.yaml) | Prior-season Ridge (alpha 10) on Elo difference, rest, rolling EPA and QB EPA; empirical residual standard deviation with Normal margin distribution, priced at an observed spread. | A small feature-layer example. In-sample residual sigma and continuous Normal probability need held-out calibration and an explicit integer push treatment. Verify spread sign conventions. |
| Team components and player availability | [nfelounits UnitModel](https://github.com/greerreNFL/nfelounits/blob/main/Model/UnitModel.py), [config](https://github.com/greerreNFL/nfelounits/blob/main/config.json) | Six pass/rush/special-teams offense/defense units with opponent/context adjustments, EWMA and QB signal. | Candidate feature architecture once starter, QB and weather histories are genuinely available as of forecast time. Do not port its weights without reproducing its input history and fit procedure. |
| Player stat and prop probabilities | [kshreyan prop projection](https://github.com/kshreyan/nfl-predict/blob/main/src/nfl/models/props/projection.py); [TMoiseyenko06 props](https://github.com/TMoiseyenko06/nfl-project/blob/claude/confident-clarke-epmijp/nflpred/features/props.py) | Former: per-stat Ridge on trailing player output and opponent allowed, Normal residual. Latter: role/share shrinkage w=n/(n+strength), example strength=4 and half-life=5, with count distributions. | Two inspectable starting points for volume/share versus direct stat regression. Neither published code result establishes a profitable historical prop strategy. Acquire genuinely timestamped lines and availability before market claims. |
| Bayesian game state / market prior | [Lopez et al. market-implied state model](https://github.com/bigfour/competitiveness/blob/master/R/jags_model_TeamHFA.bug), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.05976); [Lopez & Bliss rest model](https://github.com/ThompsonJamesBliss/rest-advantage-in-amer-football/blob/main/stan/model_no_split.stan), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10867) | Latent evolving team strength, market observations or outcomes, home/rest effects and priors. | Published statistical specifications for later challengers. Proprietary underlying odds or nonchronological validation may prevent direct replication of reported results. |

A repository existing does **not** prove it works on our decision time, market, season, or inputs. Record source revision, target, forecast timestamp, training window, missing inputs, metric, and reproducer output for every reference. Check repository licenses and transitive data terms before copying code. Algorithms and parameterization can be independently implemented; literal code copying needs a license audit.

## Current Ball Knower state and discrepancy

[team_state.py](../ball_knower_v3/modeling/team_state.py) implements Gaussian and robust Student-t-inspired team offense/defense filters; [state_fitting.py](../ball_knower_v3/modeling/state_fitting.py) selects prior/time candidates on preweek predictive play-level log densities; [game_model.py](../ball_knower_v3/modeling/game_model.py) fits direct margin and total Student-t locations with a Laplace approximation and posterior draws. [Phase 3C build report](../ball_knower_v3/PHASE3C_GAME_MODEL_BUILD_REPORT.md) reports 195 replay rows, 164 scored games in a 2025 development replay, no promoted winner, 81.1% empirical coverage for a nominal 90% margin interval, and underweight probabilities on exact margins 3 and 7. This is evidence of a current implementation and diagnostic gap, not proof that its structure is required or competitive. Earlier [v3 research roadmap](../ball_knower_v3/CHALLENGER_RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md) and [experiment contract](../ball_knower_v3/design_decisions/phase3c_prospective_experiment_contract_v1.md) remain historical records; the new work sequence is in [the research-led roadmap](RESEARCH_LED_ROADMAP.md).

## Reproduction record required for each candidate

1. Pin upstream commit/version, license, target and forecast timestamp; write out exact feature/label definitions and source code path.
2. List every input and its available-at timestamp, training span, missingness, cost, and replacements for unavailable proprietary data. Mark any deviations from upstream.
3. Reproduce published update/fitting procedure and literal default or optimized weights, with a small golden example. Do not transplant a published coefficient into different data and call it reproduced.
4. Run expanding or rolling-origin out-of-sample predictions on common games and cutoffs; tune parameters using only earlier origins, retain untouched later origins and report effective sample sizes.
5. Score win probability with log loss/Brier/calibration; margin/total with proper distribution scores and coverage plus integer-mass checks; props by market/stat with calibration and availability. Compare to trivial, market-implied (when timestamped), and reproduced reference baselines.
6. Document disagreement, sensitivity to data revisions and missing games, and decide whether the candidate is reproducible, usable as a baseline, or research only. No weights or architecture are accepted merely because an upstream repository uses them.

The open questions are which target/cutoff comes first, which market snapshots are obtainable, which published priors can be reconstructed, and which baseline wins on our shared held-out evaluation. These are experimental results to collect, not architecture decisions to preselect.
